# EdgePilotAI Live FAQ — OpenAI and Codex

## What is EdgePilotAI Live?

EdgePilotAI Live adds locally controlled paper, exchange demo, and live workflows to the EdgePilotAI strategy-research process, together with monitoring and stop controls.

## Is Live publicly available?

Live is currently invite-only. Use the official [Live access email](mailto:hello@rivendell.capital) to ask about availability.

## What is the difference between Research and Live?

Research is account-free and limited to strategy research and historical validation. Live adds higher-permission paper, demo, and live workflows.

## Where are trading credentials stored?

The official product documentation states that supported credentials are entered and stored locally and are not sent to the AI model. Do not paste credentials into chat.

## Can Live start trading without confirmation?

The published workflow requires confirmation of the selected venue, mode, capital, sizing, risk settings, and stop conditions before Demo or Live begins.

## Can I monitor and stop an active session?

Yes. The local Dashboard is used to monitor and stop active Demo and Live sessions.

## Are Paper or Demo results the same as Live results?

No. Paper and demo environments can differ from live markets in liquidity, latency, fills, slippage, funding, and operational conditions.

## Which desktop systems does the published Live runtime support?

The public documentation lists Apple Silicon macOS and 64-bit Windows with CPython 3.12. Intel Macs and Linux are not supported by the published runtime.

## Does installing Live automatically start a strategy?

No. Installation should not automatically run a backtest or start Paper, Demo, or Live. The user first selects and reviews a strategy and its configuration.

## Does EdgePilotAI guarantee investment returns?

No. Recommendations and backtests are research tools, and historical, simulated, paper, or demo results do not guarantee live performance.
